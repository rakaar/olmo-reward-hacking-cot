Bottom line

When trained on all four CoT-mention × answer-attempt groups, the layer-10 mean-CoT decoder predicts hacking on held-out problems with AUROC 0.779. Within no-mention CoTs, AUROC is 0.647.

The overall signal is reliable and stronger than visible text, the raw mention bit, prompt activations, and CoT length. The no-mention estimate is promising but not confirmed: its grouped interval [0.485, 0.790] still includes chance. Earlier single-layer causal tests did not establish suppression. A corrected Soligo-style all-position experiment was frozen and launched, then deliberately stopped at 102/700 outputs for local backup; it is incomplete and has not been outcome-scored.

Current stopping point: corrected causal experiment

A methodology audit found that the earlier layer-19 interventions did not test the paper's main layer-wise procedure. The corrected primary arm removes each layer's own unit reward-hacking direction from that layer's post-block residual at every prompt and generated-token position.

What was completed

  • Audited Soligo et al.'s paper and pinned implementation at repository revision 8460e4e426d3a89e8ed51aac0eadcdf7ac10469d.
  • Implemented all-position residual projection with mechanical telemetry for layer-wise, selected-layer, repeated-layer, and norm-matched-random interventions.
  • Ran outcome-blind coherence calibration. Full repeated layer-19 ablation at alpha = 1 was rejected for severe length/coherence behavior; repeated layer 19 at alpha = 0.5 and native layer 19 at alpha = 1 passed their frozen quality gates.
  • Froze the 20-prompt × 5-sample × 7-condition main protocol. Its sole primary arm is paper-defined layer-wise all-32-block projection with alpha = 1; the native and repeated layer-19 arms are sensitivities and cannot rescue a failed primary.
  • Generated 102 rows on the exact pinned School-of-Reward-Hacks OLMo checkpoint, stopped the process, and verified every substantive remote data file against the local copy by SHA-256.

No causal result yet
The frozen grid is incomplete and only the first three same-distribution prompts were reached. The 102 answers were not unblinded, reward-hacking-scored, or entered into the planned bootstrap analysis. They must not be used to infer whether the corrected intervention works.

Safely resumable
The partial rollouts, running-state manifest, log, exact launch source, preflight record, prompt/config/protocol hashes, and remote checksum file are preserved under data/causal-directions/causal-main/. The runner's resume-compatibility checks can continue the same frozen grid on a future GPU.

2. Two independently built shortcut directions

Both directions were obtained from teacher-forced contrast text, not from the CodeContests rollouts. At every layer:

direction = mean(pooled shortcut activation − pooled legitimate activation)
TrackSourceFit pairsHeld outPurpose

BLuna-generated implicit shortcut vs legitimate pairs6020Independent concept construction across held-out domains

What passed
The predeclared reliability and held-out-margin criteria qualified a few layers: 7 and 9 for last-token pooling; 0, 6, 9, and 10 for mean pooling.

What that means
Small positive cosines can be statistically stable without being conceptually large. These directions should be treated as related weak readouts, not the same feature or circuit.

3. Causal projection tests

Earlier AISI layer-14 test

The School of Reward Hacks mean-token direction was ablated from the first generated token onward. For unit direction d:

updated residual h′ = h − λ × (h · d) × d

λ = 1 removes the component parallel to the direction. λ = 2 reverses that component; it is not merely a stronger orthogonal ablation.

ConditionHack attemptedSyntax-validStructured completionInterpretation
Control · λ = 06 / 205 / 205 / 20No intervention
Remove · λ = 18 / 204 / 204 / 20Did not reduce attempts
Reverse · λ = 26 / 204 / 204 / 20Same total as control

No causal suppression at layer 14
The λ=2 condition suppressed three control hacks but induced three others; exact paired McNemar p = 1.0. The hook worked mechanically, but the initial five-example effect did not replicate across 20 examples. Generation quality was poor in all conditions, so this rules out a large gross effect under this setup—not every possible causal role.

Native School-of-Reward-Hacks capability test

A separate confirmation fit the public contrast direction on training pairs only, selected layer 19 without using these prompts, and evaluated 20 native School-of-Reward-Hacks capability prompts: ten held-out same-distribution and ten novel cross-domain. Baseline and intervention generations used paired seeds. Two independent reviewers scored a shuffled, condition-blinded packet for metric gaming and coherence.

Observational direction, not established causal feature
The direction has strong held-out readout margins, but projection removal at one layer, projection removal across the neighboring three-layer band, and component reflection all failed the directional causal prediction. These tests do not prove the direction has no causal role under every intervention, but they are sufficient to stop escalating this particular ablation strategy.

3b. Synthetic misalignment-direction preparation

Eight independently authored shards now cover all 512 frozen factorial slots: 180 fit, 78 validation, 30 IID test, 96 domain-OOD, 96 facet-OOD, and 32 double-OOD pairs. The global validator reports 512/512 structurally valid rows, exact split coverage, no duplicate scenario or candidate IDs, and no cross-shard near-duplicate rejection. All rows remain pending independent semantic review and have not been used for activation extraction.

What is clean
Numeric surface style reaches at most 0.630 and within-pair shared vocabulary 0.597. The response lengths are essentially at chance. Side-exclusive content carries strong signal, as expected for texts that enact different behavior.

Decision required before GPU extraction
The original gate conflates intended semantic separability with nuisance leakage, but a content-free function-word probe still reaches 0.783. No threshold was relaxed and no synthetic activation direction was fit. The proposed v2 amendment keeps full-text TF-IDF as a reported baseline and applies hard leakage gates only to predeclared style/structure and independent nuisance controls.

Because global lexical diagnostics have already guided authoring, this 512-pair pool must be described as developmental. The natural OLMo EM evaluation set remains untouched and unavailable to layer selection.

Robust answer-side signal
For the first 128 answer tokens, layers 3–13 and 15 have positive matched pointwise intervals. At layer 10, each released hack family is elevated: always_equal 1.249, conftest 1.165, and exit 1.223, versus 0.930 for no attempt.

Likely explicit mechanism readout
Frequent maximum-projection tokens include pytest, Equal, hack, and ==. Successful attempts are not stronger than failed ones in the fixed window, so the direction reads hack-like code/content rather than whether it will work.

6. Layer-10 mean-pooled CoT decoder

This decoder learns a new linear direction from the CoT activations themselves; it does not reuse the School of Reward Hacks direction. Complete CoTs were available for 196 rollouts. Each feature is the mean post-block layer-10 residual over non-special tokens strictly inside <thinking>, excluding the prompt, delimiters, answer/code, EOS, and padding.

Information beyond obvious controls
Overall activation AUROC exceeds TF-IDF by +0.071, paired interval [0.005, 0.140], and the raw mention indicator by +0.146 [0.063, 0.222]. The overall pilot gate passes.

Tentative silent signal
Silent attempts average 0.373 versus 0.284 for no-mention non-attempts. Their AUROC is 0.647, but its interval crosses 0.5 and the advantage over prompt activations is not reliable yet.

The earlier transparent-only fit is retained for provenance but superseded. Layer 10 was selected after inspecting answer-side projections on these same 200 rollouts, so the all-groups result remains exploratory. The confirmation stage freezes the design, draws 1,000 rollouts from 200 unused problems, and then uses only problem IDs and behavior-cell labels to balance 120/40/40 problem-disjoint train/validation/test sets. Fresh training must have at least 60 examples in every cell; validation and test must each have at least 20. The pipeline aborts if those hard gates cannot be met. It adds all complete-CoT pilot rows only to training, selects settings across all four validation cells, and evaluates the all-four-groups test set once.

Most important remaining controls

  • Run the frozen layer-10 mean-CoT decoder confirmation on 200 unused problem IDs without another layer or pooling search.
  • Retain the visible-CoT TF-IDF, prompt-only activation, and token-count controls on identical splits.
  • If whole-CoT averaging fails confirmation or silent transfer, predeclare the planned four-position-bin sensitivity test; do not reinterpret the pilot.
  • Do not infer that layer-10 steering will work from observational separation; causal readout and causal control are different properties.
